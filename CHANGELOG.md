# Changelog

## 0.2.2

Swaying trees in Toy Town and beyond.

- The redrawn cherry and yard trees in Toy Town sway in the wind again.
- Snowy firs, bamboo and the cherry and zelkova trees in Highton Terrace and Odaiba now sway too; bamboo bends from its base.

## 0.2.1

Night details, a calmer soundscape and a Linux client.

- New night lights: the dragon's eye in Machine City, the Factory Town generators (also by day), the Kaiser's Fortress incubators, Sunset Bay's tiki torches and its lighthouse with a turning beam.
- Hanging pumpkin lamps glow at the pumpkin; Piedmon's castle windows glow more softly.
- Map exits glow at night with rising sparks.
- Chimneys in Gate Village smoke in autumn and winter.
- Water, sea and waterfalls are quieter with less rumble; creeks are softly audible from farther away.
- Savanna acacias sway without gaps in their crowns.
- Simpler audio settings: the technical compressor controls are gone.
- Windows, lights and headlights follow the game's redrawn art in Toy Town, Odaiba, Machine City and Fog City.
- The new Tamers maps get city or nature ambience, matching weather, car headlights and glowing Hypnos towers.
- Block piles cast one shadow from their base, and narrow pier ends show a reflection again.
- New Linux client (AppImage) with automatic updates.

## 0.2

A large atmosphere update: classic water reflections, natural tree movement and many new lights.

- Water mirrors characters downward at ponds, lakes and beaches.
- Leaves and crowns sway while trunks stay put; palms bend from the base.
- Lanterns, candles and chandeliers glow from their flames and glass; more windows light up at night, including Toy Town, Machine City and Piedmon's castle.
- New night details: the Destiny Lake wreck, TV portals and Factory sludge.
- Attacks, skill projectiles and beams cast a matching light at night.
- Shadows no longer fall across houses and objects.
- Clouds and weather start at 2x, seasonal leaves follow the weather setting, and Primary Village has livelier confetti.
- Water sounds at ponds and lakes, waves on the Fog City coast, regional wind and muffled loudspeaker chatter in Server Desert.
- Atmosphere clock: real time, a repeating day or a fixed time (F10).
- Player night light starts at 1.05x; other saved settings are kept.
- The desktop client shows DigiMMO as your game in Discord.

## 0.1.4

Calmer birds and distant city traffic.

- Bird calls are quieter and shorter, with longer pauses and varied passages; the continuous bird loop is gone.
- Distant daytime traffic replaces the repeating city market voices and fades into quiet wind at night.

## 0.1.3

More window lights, a new campfire sound and a lasting update notice.

- Windows light up in Fog City's hall and street buildings; a few windows and broken panes glow in Machine City at night.
- South-facing headlights follow the car body.
- Weather starts at 2x; Network and its dungeons have no clouds or cloud shadows, and Holy Peaks snow fades past the northern plateau.
- A new campfire recording with a gentle low cut keeps its natural crackle; fountains fade out over a shorter distance.
- A notice appears when a client update is ready and returns after reloading the game.
- The development client uses the DigiMMO name and the official Windows icon, and both clients have their own taskbar identity.

## 0.1.2

Menu fixes for smaller windows and a smaller download.

- Settings and keyboard shortcut help stay inside the menu on smaller windows.
- Client updates include the browser extension and userscript folders.
- The ZIP download is smaller. Users of the old standalone 0.1.1 EXE should switch to the ZIP once.

## 0.1.1

A portable client with automatic updates.

- Download the portable ZIP from the release assets, extract it and run DigiMMO.exe; no installation required. The green Source code ZIP is not the game client.
- The folder includes the optional userscript and the Chrome/Edge extension beside the EXE.
- Automatic updates apply after you close the client, including existing Setup installations.

## 0.1

The first release: light, shadows and ambience.

- Optional lighting, object shadows, moving treetops and water reflections.
- Regional day and night ambience with wildlife, fire, fountains and nearby water.
- Larger Digimon cast wider light, moonlight shadows are softer, and the atmosphere starts as soon as the map is ready.
- See the [known issues](https://github.com/dadero-deadline/digimmo-client#known-issues). The Windows installer is unsigned; browser ZIPs and the userscript update manually.
