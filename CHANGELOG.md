# Changelog

## 0.2 (unreleased)

A large atmosphere update: classic water reflections, natural tree movement and many new lights.

### Lighting
- Lanterns, candles and chandeliers glow from their flames and glass.
- More windows light up at night, including Toy Town, Machine City and Piedmon's castle.
- New night details: the Destiny Lake wreck, TV portals and Factory sludge.
- Attacks, skill projectiles and beams cast a matching light at night.

### Shadows and reflections
- Water mirrors characters downward at ponds, lakes and beaches.
- Shadows no longer fall across houses and objects.

### Trees and weather
- Leaves and crowns sway while trunks stay put; palms bend from the base.
- Clouds and weather start at 2x; seasonal leaves follow the weather setting.
- Livelier confetti in Primary Village.

### Ambience
- Water sounds at ponds and lakes, waves on the Fog City coast and wind that varies by region.
- Muffled loudspeaker chatter in Server Desert.

### Settings and client
- Atmosphere clock: real time, a repeating day or a fixed time (F10).
- Player night light starts at 1.05x; other saved settings are kept.
- The desktop client shows DigiMMO as your game in Discord.

## 0.1.4

- Quieter, shorter bird calls with longer pauses and varied passages. The continuous bird loop has been removed.
- Distant daytime traffic replaces the repeating city market voices and fades into quiet wind at night.

## 0.1.3

- New campfire recording with a gentle low cut; natural crackle dynamics are preserved. Fountains fade out over a shorter distance.
- Weather defaults to 2x. Network and its dungeon variants have no clouds or cloud shadows. Holy Peaks snow fades naturally beyond the northern plateau.
- A persistent client notice appears when an update is ready and returns after reloading the game.
- Window lighting for Fog City's hall and street buildings.
- South-facing headlights follow the car body. A few windows and broken panes glow at night in Machine City.
- DigiMMO name and official Windows icon for the development client, with explicit taskbar identity in both clients.

## 0.1.2

- Settings and keyboard shortcut help fit inside the menu on smaller windows.
- Client updates include the browser extension and userscript folders.
- Smaller ZIP download. Users of the old standalone 0.1.1 EXE should switch to the ZIP once.

## 0.1.1

- Download the portable release asset, extract it and run DigiMMO.exe. No installation required. The repo's green Source code ZIP is not the game client.
- The ZIP is a normal game folder, with the optional userscript and Chrome/Edge extension beside the EXE.
- Automatic updates apply after you close the client, including existing Setup installations.

## 0.1

- [New] Optional lighting, object shadows, moving treetops and water reflections.
- [New] Regional day and night ambience with wildlife, fire, fountains and nearby water.
- [New] Wider light for larger Digimon, softer moonlight shadows and atmosphere when the map is ready.

See [known issues](https://github.com/dadero-deadline/digimmo-client#known-issues). The Windows installer is unsigned; browser ZIPs and the userscript require manual updates.
